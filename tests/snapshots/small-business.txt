If you consider that the Privacy Act does not apply to you, for example
because you are a small business operator under section 6D, please tell me
so in writing. Under section 6D(4)(c) and (d), an individual, body

business operator if it discloses personal information about another
individual to anyone else for a benefit, service or advantage, or provides a
benefit, service or advantage to collect such information from anyone else.
Section 6D(5) to (8) set out exceptions, including for some conduct outside
the course of a business and for such a disclosure or collection made with
the individual's consent or as required or authorised by or under
legislation.
